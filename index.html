About the Project

A budget planner helps users organize and manage their finances effectively. Users can input income and expenses, set savings goals, and categorize expenditures.

The platform provides visualizations, such as pie charts and graphs, to highlight spending patterns. Alerts for overspending and monthly summaries ensure better financial discipline and planning.
